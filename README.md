## 🌠 About Me

Imagine a pond buried beneath mud, waste, and debris. Its true beauty remains hidden until someone clears the mess, restores its balance, and creates space for life to flourish.

📊 **I see data the same way.** Raw data can be inconsistent, incomplete, and overwhelming. I enjoy bringing structure to that complexity—cleaning datasets, uncovering hidden patterns, and turning raw information into meaningful business insights.

- 🧹 **My approach:** Clean the data. Discover the patterns. Communicate the story. Support better decisions.
- 🎯 **My focus:** Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, business intelligence, KPI reporting, and dashboard development.
- 🛠️ **My toolkit:** Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and Excel.
- 🤝 **Collaboration:** Interested in data analytics and business intelligence projects.
- 💡 **My philosophy:** Messy data can be transformed into clarity, insight, and opportunity.
